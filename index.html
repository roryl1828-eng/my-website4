HOW TO PLAY

PLAYER 1

A D Move / double tap dash

W Jump • S Crouch

F Punch • G Kick

R Heavy • T Special

E Tag • Q Block

PLAYER 2

← → Move / double tap dash

↑ Jump • ↓ Crouch

J Punch • K Kick

U Heavy • O Special

L Tag • I Block

Crouch + kick = sweep. Crouch + heavy = launcher. Punch → kick → heavy chains a combo. Jump + attack = aerial. Hold block + attack for a counter. Tag during a hit combo or hold forward + tag for a tag strike. Specials use energy; attacks and blocks charge it. Defeat both rivals to win a round. Highest combined health wins at time-out. First to 2 rounds wins. Esc pauses.
